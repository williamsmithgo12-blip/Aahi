The Aahii Experience

Where heritage meets modern sophistication.

Opening its doors in late 2025, Aahii is poised to become a destination for sophisticated North Indian dining. This restaurant and bar offers a modern interpretation of familiar regional flavours, complemented by expertly crafted cocktails.

Under the guidance of renowned chefs, the menu features a refined selection of slow-cooked kebabs, fragrant biryanis, and comforting mains — alongside playful small plates that reinvent classic Indian street food.
